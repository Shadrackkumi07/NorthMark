Northmark Facility Services delivers three related service lines to commercial facilities in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead: recurring commercial cleaning and janitorial service, commercial property maintenance, and aerial drone inspection and documentation. Every service runs on the same operating model: a walkthrough, a written scope, background-checked staff, documented access control and supervisor QA. One relationship can cover the daily care of a building, its routine upkeep and the documentation of its condition.

## How our services fit together

Most facility vendors do one thing. That is fine until a property manager is juggling a cleaning company, a handyman, a roofer for inspections and a photographer for condition reports, each with different standards, invoices and points of contact. Northmark's services are designed to connect.

- **Cleaning keeps the building usable.** Restrooms, workspaces, breakrooms and entries are cleaned on a fixed schedule against a written checklist.
- **Maintenance keeps it in good condition.** Routine upkeep and condition checks catch small problems before they become tenant complaints or capital expenses.
- **Aerial documentation records how it is doing.** Roof, facade and site imagery gives owners, managers and insurers an objective record over time.

Because our cleaning teams are already in the building on a schedule, they often notice maintenance issues first: a water stain, a running toilet, a failing door closer. Those observations feed straight into maintenance work instead of waiting for someone to complain.

## What every service includes

Regardless of which service you buy, you get:

1. **A walkthrough before pricing.** We quote what we have seen, not what we guess. [Schedule a facility walkthrough](/facility-walkthrough).
2. **A written scope.** Every task, area and frequency in writing.
3. **Background-checked staff.** Every team member is screened before receiving access.
4. **Insurance and bonding.** Certificates are available on request.
5. **Documented access control.** Keys, codes and lock-up procedures are agreed and logged. See [security and access control](/security-and-access-control).
6. **Supervisor QA.** Work is checked and scored by a supervisor, not discovered by your staff. See our [quality assurance program](/quality-assurance-program).
7. **One accountable contact.** You report an issue once.

## What we do not do

Being clear about scope is part of being accountable. Northmark does not provide residential housekeeping. We also do not market specialty services we do not deliver. If you need something outside our scope, we will tell you so rather than subcontract it without your knowledge.
