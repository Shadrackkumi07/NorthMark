## What to include when you contact us

A few details help us respond with something useful on the first reply:

- **Your building:** address or city, type of facility and approximate square footage
- **What you need:** recurring cleaning, janitorial service, property maintenance, aerial inspection or a combination
- **Timing:** whether you need to start by a specific date, for example at the end of a current vendor's notice period
- **Requirements:** insurance limits, background check rules, after-hours access or special areas

If you are a property manager with several buildings, list them all. We can scope a portfolio together, which is usually faster for you and produces consistent pricing across sites. See [multi-site janitorial](/services/multi-site-janitorial).

## Common reasons people contact us

### Requesting a quote

Most new clients contact us because they need a proposal. We will set up a [facility walkthrough](/facility-walkthrough), write a scope and send pricing. If you want to understand pricing first, read our [commercial cleaning cost guide](/resources/commercial-cleaning-cost-fargo).

### Replacing a cleaning vendor

If your current provider is missing visits or not responding, tell us your notice period and we will plan around it. Our guide to [switching janitorial companies](/resources/switching-janitorial-companies) covers the handover.

### Certificates of insurance

Property managers and corporate compliance teams can request certificates of insurance by email. Include the certificate holder's legal name, address and any additional insured requirements. See [insured and bonded](/insured-and-bonded).

### Aerial inspection requests

For roof overviews, hail documentation or site condition imagery, tell us the property address, what you need documented and your deadline. See [aerial inspection and documentation](/services/aerial-inspection-documentation).

### Existing clients

If you are an existing client, contact your account lead directly, or call the main number and we will route your request. Issues are logged and owned until they are resolved and confirmed with you.

### Employment

If you are looking for work, see our [careers](/careers) page rather than the main contact line, so your application reaches the right person.

## Before you call: a quick self-check

If you are not sure what to ask for, these questions usually clarify it:

1. Is the problem that the building is not clean, or that nobody checks whether it is?
2. Do you need someone in the building every night, or a few nights a week?
3. Are there areas that only certain people should enter?
4. Do you also need routine upkeep, or roof and site documentation?
5. Do you need certificates of insurance for a landlord or owner?

Bring the answers to the call and we can usually recommend a starting scope in a few minutes.
