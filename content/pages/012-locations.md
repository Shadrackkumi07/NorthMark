Northmark Facility Services serves commercial facilities in the three core cities of the Fargo–Moorhead metro: Fargo and West Fargo in North Dakota, and Moorhead in Minnesota. We keep our service area deliberately focused. That focus is what lets supervisors inspect buildings in person, keeps response times short and means our teams know the local building stock, the weather and the way business works on both sides of the Red River.

## Why we stay focused on three cities

Many cleaning companies list dozens of towns on their websites. Some of those lists are aspirational, and in practice a building 60 miles away gets less supervision, slower response and crews who drive further between sites. We would rather serve three cities well than many cities thinly.

A focused area also makes our quality model work. Our [quality assurance program](/quality-assurance-program) depends on supervisors walking buildings regularly. That is realistic across Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead. It would not be across half of eastern North Dakota and western Minnesota.

## Fargo

Fargo is the region's commercial center: downtown offices and banks, the medical campuses along the I-94 and 45th Street corridors, retail along 13th Avenue South, and a growing number of new commercial buildings. The city issued 61 new commercial building permits valued at about $196 million for January–September 2025. Start with our [Fargo commercial cleaning](/locations/fargo-nd) page.

## West Fargo

West Fargo combines fast-growing residential neighborhoods with a substantial industrial and distribution base, plus new commercial development along its main corridors. Warehouses, contractors, equipment dealers and light industrial users make up a large share of its commercial building stock. See [West Fargo commercial cleaning](/locations/west-fargo-nd).

## Moorhead

Moorhead sits across the Red River in Minnesota, home to Minnesota State University Moorhead and Concordia College, with professional offices, healthcare, retail and municipal buildings. Serving Moorhead means working within Minnesota's employment and business rules, which we account for. See [Moorhead commercial cleaning](/locations/moorhead-mn).

## How we plan routes and supervision

Service quality in janitorial work depends heavily on logistics. Teams that drive long distances between buildings arrive late, rush and leave early. Supervisors who cover a huge territory inspect less often. By keeping our area to the three core cities, we can build efficient nightly routes, keep supervisors close to every building they oversee, and respond quickly when a client calls about a problem or an after-hours incident. That is a practical decision about doing the work well, not a marketing line.

## Working across the state line

Fargo–Moorhead is one metro split between two states. Many property managers and businesses own or lease space on both sides of the river. Northmark serves both, so a portfolio that includes a Fargo office and a Moorhead clinic can run on one scope format, one QA program and one point of contact. ## Frequently asked questions

### What cities does Northmark serve?

Northmark serves commercial facilities in Fargo and West Fargo, North Dakota, and Moorhead, Minnesota.

### Do you serve towns outside those three cities?

Our service area is Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead. If your facility is nearby, contact us and we will tell you honestly whether we can supervise it properly.

### Can one contract cover buildings in both North Dakota and Minnesota?

Yes. We serve both sides of the Red River and can manage a cross-state portfolio under one scope format and one account contact.

### Is pricing different between cities?

Pricing depends on the building and scope, not the city. Travel within our service area does not change how we price.
