Northmark's resource library is written for the people who buy and manage facility services: office managers, practice administrators, property managers, operations leads and building owners in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead. Every guide answers a real question we hear from buyers, cites public sources such as OSHA, the EPA, the CDC, the FAA, the Census Bureau and the Bureau of Labor Statistics, and separates general guidance from the scope agreed for an individual building. You do not need to be a Northmark client to use them.

## Why we publish these guides

Commercial cleaning is a confusing market. The same words mean different things to different vendors. "Disinfection" can mean a quick wipe or an EPA-registered product used at the right contact time. "Bonded" can be printed on a van without any bond behind it. Price quotes vary wildly because vendors assume different scopes. Buyers end up comparing proposals that are not comparable.

These guides exist to fix that. They explain how commercial cleaning, janitorial service, property maintenance and aerial inspection actually work, what drives cost, what regulations apply, and what questions to ask any vendor, including us. A better-informed buyer is good for honest vendors.

## Our editorial standards

Every guide in this library follows a few rules:

1. **Answer first.** The first paragraph answers the question directly. Detail follows.
2. **Cite public sources.** Regulations link to the agency that publishes them. Statistics link to the agency that produced them.
3. **No invented numbers.** We do not publish made-up statistics, fake testimonials or fabricated case studies. Where we give illustrative numbers, such as a sample cost calculation, we label them as illustrations.
4. **Honest about limits.** We say clearly when a question needs a lawyer, accountant, engineer, roofer or adjuster rather than a cleaning company.
5. **Kept current.** Each guide shows its publication date and, when revised, an update date. Regulations and local data change, and we update guides when they do.
