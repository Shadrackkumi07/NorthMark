## What commercial cleaning means at Northmark

"Commercial cleaning" is a broad phrase. Search for it and you will find carpet companies, residential maids who also take office jobs, and national franchises. We use the term narrowly: **routine, scheduled cleaning of occupied commercial buildings**, performed by the same trained team on a fixed scope, with written records of what was done.

That scope usually includes:

- Workspaces and offices: trash and recycling removal, dusting of reachable horizontal surfaces, wiping desks that have been cleared, vacuuming carpeted areas and damp-mopping hard floors.
- Restrooms: cleaning fixtures, mirrors and partitions, emptying waste, and reporting low supplies or maintenance problems to your contact.
- Breakrooms and kitchens: counters, sinks, exterior appliance surfaces, tables and floors.
- Entrances, lobbies and corridors: glass doors, entry floors, reception counters and high-traffic touchpoints such as door handles and elevator buttons.
- Conference rooms and shared areas: tables, chairs, whiteboard trays and floors reset for the next business day.

Every building gets its own written scope. A dental clinic, a 40-person accounting office and a distribution center's office wing should not run on the same checklist, and on our accounts they don't.

## How a Northmark cleaning program runs

### 1. Walkthrough and written scope

We start by walking your building with you. We note square footage, floor types, restroom count, occupancy, security requirements, after-hours access rules and the areas that matter most to you. You get a written scope that lists each task and how often it happens. Nothing is left to "we'll take care of it."

### 2. Secure onboarding

Before the first visit we agree on how our team enters and leaves: keys, fobs, alarm codes, sign-in procedures and who to call if something looks wrong. Our [security and access control](/security-and-access-control) standards cover key logs, code handling and lock-up checks. Only background-checked staff assigned to your account are given access.

### 3. Checklist-driven visits

Each visit is completed against your site checklist. Cleaners log completion, note anything unusual (a leaking faucet, a broken light, a door that would not latch) and report it to you rather than leaving it for you to discover.

### 4. Supervisor QA walkthroughs

A supervisor inspects completed work on a recurring schedule, scores it against the scope and corrects gaps before they become complaints. Our [quality assurance program](/quality-assurance-program) explains how those inspections are scored and how issues are closed out.

### 5. One point of contact

You have a direct line to the person responsible for your account. If something is missed, you report it once and we fix it. That is the core of how we work.

### Do you provide after-hours commercial cleaning?

Yes. Most of our commercial cleaning happens after hours so your staff and customers are not disrupted. We agree access, alarm and lock-up procedures in writing before the first visit.

Yes. Every Northmark team member is background-checked before receiving access to any client building, and Northmark carries insurance and bonding.

### How often should my building be cleaned?

Most offices need service between three and five nights a week. High-traffic, medical or customer-facing spaces often need nightly service. We recommend a frequency during the walkthrough based on occupancy and use.
