Commercial janitorial service is the routine, recurring work that keeps an occupied building clean and functional day after day: trash and recycling, restrooms, floors, dusting, breakrooms and entries, done on a fixed schedule by the same trained team. Northmark provides commercial janitorial services to facilities in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead, with a written scope, background-checked staff, documented access control and supervisor QA walkthroughs on every account.

## Janitorial service is a system, not a visit

Janitorial work is repetitive by design. The same restrooms need cleaning tonight, tomorrow and next Tuesday. The value of a janitorial provider is not in any single visit. It is in **consistency over hundreds of visits**: the same tasks done to the same standard by people who know the building.

That is why searches for "commercial janitorial company" and "commercial janitorial cleaning services" are really searches for reliability. Northmark's answer is structural:

- A **written scope** so the tasks never drift
- **Assigned teams** so the people know the building
- **Checklists** completed on every visit
- **Supervisor walkthroughs** that score the work
- **One account contact** who owns problems until they are fixed

## What a janitorial scope typically includes

Every building gets its own scope, but most commercial janitorial programs are built from these tasks:

| Area | Typical nightly tasks | Typical weekly or periodic tasks |
|---|---|---|
| Workspaces | Empty trash and recycling, spot-clean glass, vacuum traffic lanes | Dust reachable surfaces, detail vacuum edges and under desks |
| Restrooms | Clean and disinfect fixtures, mirrors, counters; empty waste; mop floors | Detail partitions, wash walls around fixtures, clean vents |
| Breakrooms | Clean counters, sinks, tables, appliance exteriors; mop floors | Clean inside microwaves and fridge exteriors in detail |
| Entries and lobbies | Clean door glass, vacuum or mop entry floors, wipe reception counters | Detail baseboards, clean walk-off mats |
| Corridors and stairs | Vacuum or dust-mop, spot-clean walls and handrails | Detail stair edges and corners |
| Conference rooms | Wipe tables, reset chairs, empty trash, vacuum | Detail chairs and baseboards |

## Choosing the right frequency

Frequency is the biggest single driver of both cleanliness and cost. Common options are:

- **Five nights a week** for busy offices, clinics, banks and customer-facing spaces
- **Three nights a week** for moderate-traffic offices where restrooms and trash can wait a day
- **One or two nights a week** for small, low-occupancy offices
- **Split schedules**, for example restrooms and trash nightly but full vacuuming twice a week

We recommend a frequency during the walkthrough and explain why. ## Safety and chemical handling

Janitorial work involves cleaning chemicals, wet floors and lifting. OSHA's Hazard Communication standard requires that employees have access to safety data sheets and are trained on the hazards of the chemicals they use. Northmark team members are trained on the products used at each site, and wet floors are signed during and after mopping. If you have specific product requirements, such as fragrance-free products in a clinic or products approved by your organization, we build them into the scope.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is commercial janitorial service?

Commercial janitorial service is recurring cleaning of an occupied commercial building, including trash removal, restroom cleaning, floor care, dusting and breakroom cleaning, performed on a fixed schedule against a written scope.

### How many nights a week do we need janitorial service?

Most busy offices and clinics need five nights a week. Moderate-traffic offices often do well with three. We recommend a frequency after walking your building.

### Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Our teams bring the equipment and cleaning products needed for the scope. Consumables such as paper towels and soap are handled as agreed in your contract.

### Can janitorial service start quickly?

Yes, once the scope, access procedures and first-visit date are agreed. If you are switching providers, we plan around your current notice period.
