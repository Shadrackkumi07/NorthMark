Facility cleaning is janitorial service for buildings that mix several kinds of space under one roof: offices next to a shop floor, a showroom in front of a parts warehouse, classrooms beside a gym, a lobby leading to production. Northmark cleans these facilities in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead by **zoning** the building and writing a separate scope for each zone, so every area gets the right tasks, frequency and safety rules instead of one generic checklist.

## Why facility cleaning is different from office cleaning

An office cleaning checklist assumes carpeted workspaces, a breakroom and a couple of restrooms. Many Fargo–Moorhead commercial buildings look nothing like that. The region's economy leans on distribution, manufacturing, agriculture and equipment, so it is common to find:

- A dealership with a showroom, sales offices, a service shop and a parts room
- A distribution center with a two-story office wing, driver lounges and a large warehouse floor
- A light industrial building with offices, a lab and assembly areas
- A church or school with classrooms, offices, a kitchen and a gymnasium

Each of those zones gets dirty differently, is used on a different schedule and carries different safety considerations. Cleaning them all on one checklist means either over-cleaning some areas or neglecting others.

## How we zone a facility

During the [facility walkthrough](/facility-walkthrough) we split the building into zones and document each one:

| Zone type | Common characteristics | Typical janitorial focus |
|---|---|---|
| Office and administrative | Carpet, desks, conference rooms | Trash, dusting, vacuuming, touchpoints |
| Customer-facing | Showrooms, lobbies, waiting areas | Glass, entry floors, seating, presentation |
| Restrooms and locker rooms | High use, hard surfaces | Fixtures, floors, waste, supply reporting |
| Breakrooms and driver lounges | Food prep, shared seating | Counters, sinks, tables, appliance exteriors, floors |
| Shop, warehouse or production support | Sealed concrete, equipment, traffic | Sweeping or dust-mopping accessible floor areas, trash, aisle debris |
| Storage and mechanical | Low traffic | Periodic sweeping and trash only, if in scope |

Each zone gets its own task list, frequency and access rules. A restroom used by a 40-person shop crew may need nightly attention, while a mezzanine storage area needs a monthly sweep.

## Safety in non-office zones

Production and warehouse areas bring hazards an office does not. OSHA's walking-working surfaces standard requires that floors be kept clean, orderly and, as far as practical, free of hazards such as spills and debris. OSHA's sanitation standard sets requirements for workplace toilet facilities and washrooms. Our scopes support both, but we do not take over responsibilities that belong to your safety program.

In these zones our teams:

- Follow your site safety rules, including PPE, marked walkways and restricted areas
- Stay clear of operating equipment and never clean machinery or anything under lockout
- Clean only accessible floor areas, not inside racking or around active equipment
- Report spills, leaks or hazards to your designated contact

## Scheduling around operations

Facilities that run multiple shifts may not have a quiet window. We schedule zone by zone: offices after administrative staff leave, restrooms and breakrooms between shifts, and customer-facing areas before opening. The schedule is written into your scope, and supervisors check it during QA walkthroughs.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is facility janitorial cleaning?

Facility janitorial cleaning is recurring cleaning of a commercial building that contains several kinds of space, such as offices, shops, warehouses or public areas, with each zone cleaned to its own scope and schedule.

### Do you clean production equipment?

No. Production equipment and anything under lockout stays with your trained staff. We clean offices, restrooms, breakrooms, locker rooms, customer areas and accessible floor areas.

### Can you work around multiple shifts?

Yes. We schedule each zone around its own usage, for example offices after hours and restrooms between shifts.

### Do you follow our site safety rules?

Yes. Our teams follow your PPE requirements, walkway markings and restricted-area rules, and report hazards to your designated contact.
