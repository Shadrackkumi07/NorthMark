After-hours janitorial service means your building is cleaned once staff, patients and customers have gone home, typically in the evening or overnight, so cleaning never disrupts the workday. Northmark provides after-hours janitorial service across Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead. Because our teams work in empty buildings with your keys and alarm codes, every after-hours account includes written access procedures, logged credentials and a lock-up check before the team leaves.

## Why most commercial cleaning happens after hours

The Bureau of Labor Statistics notes that janitors and building cleaners often work evenings and nights, because offices and commercial buildings are usually cleaned when they are empty. There are good reasons for that:

- **No disruption.** Vacuums, floor machines and restroom closures do not interrupt meetings, phone calls or patient visits.
- **Better results.** Desks, floors and restrooms can be cleaned thoroughly without working around people.
- **Faster work.** Empty buildings let teams follow an efficient route instead of waiting for rooms to clear.
- **Ready mornings.** The building is clean when the first employee arrives.

The trade-off is trust. An after-hours cleaning team is often the only outside party in your building at night. That makes how the vendor handles access as important as how well it cleans.

## How we handle after-hours access

### Before the first visit

We document, in writing:

- Which background-checked team members are assigned to your building
- How keys or fobs are issued, tagged and logged
- Alarm arming and disarming procedures, and individual codes where your system supports them
- Which rooms are restricted, and under what conditions they may be entered
- Your lock-up checklist: exterior doors, interior doors, lights, windows and alarm
- Who our team calls, and in what order, if something is wrong

### During each visit

- Arrival and departure are recorded
- The team follows the site checklist and enters only scoped areas
- Doors found propped or unlocked are secured and noted
- Anything unusual is reported through the agreed procedure

### At the end of each visit

The lock-up checklist is completed before the team leaves. If a door will not latch or the alarm will not arm, the team follows your escalation procedure instead of leaving the building unsecured.

Full details are on our [security and access control](/security-and-access-control) page. For a vendor-neutral guide to setting these expectations, read [vendor key and access control](/resources/vendor-key-and-access-control).

## Typical after-hours schedules

| Building type | Common after-hours window | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Offices | 6 p.m. to 11 p.m. | Most common; aligns with staff departures |
| Medical and dental | After the last patient, often 6 p.m. to 10 p.m. | Coordinated with clinical close-out |
| Banks and credit unions | After branch close | Coordinated with vault and cash procedures |
| Retail | After close or early morning before opening | Early mornings suit stores open late |
| [Restaurants](/industries/restaurants) | After close or pre-open | Front-of-house and restrooms; kitchen cleaning stays with staff |
| Multi-shift facilities | Between shifts by zone | See [facility cleaning](/services/facility-cleaning) |

We fit the window to your building, not to our convenience. If you need early-morning service so the building is freshly cleaned at opening, we can schedule it.

## Who needs after-hours cleaning most

- **[Banks and credit unions](/industries/banks-credit-unions):** cleaning must happen when branches are closed and fit within strict security procedures.
- **[Medical offices](/industries/medical-offices):** patient areas are cleaned after the last appointment, with privacy expectations and clinical boundaries agreed in writing.
- **Professional offices:** law, accounting and insurance firms prefer cleaning when confidential work is not in progress.
- **Customer-facing businesses:** retail and service businesses want spotless spaces at opening.

## Safety for our team

After-hours work also raises safety considerations for the people doing it. OSHA's workplace violence resources point employers toward practical controls for workers who work alone or at night. We plan for that: teams know who to call, how to handle unexpected visitors and when to leave a building and call for help. A safe team is a reliable team.

## What if something goes wrong overnight?

Things happen at night: a pipe bursts, an alarm trips, a door is found forced. During onboarding you decide what our team does in each case and who gets called. Incidents are documented and reported to you, usually before you would otherwise have known.

## Frequently asked questions

### What time do after-hours cleaners usually arrive?

Most office cleaning starts between 5 p.m. and 7 p.m., after staff leave. We set the window around your building's schedule, including early-morning service if you prefer.

### Will the same people clean our building each night?

Yes. After-hours accounts have assigned, background-checked team members, and we tell you about any changes.

### How do you make sure the building is locked when you leave?

Every visit ends with a site-specific lock-up checklist covering doors, lights, windows and alarm arming. Problems follow your escalation procedure.

### Can you clean while some staff are still working late?

Yes. Teams work around late staff by cleaning occupied areas last or returning to them before leaving.
