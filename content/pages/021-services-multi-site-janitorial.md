Northmark's multi-site janitorial service gives organizations with several buildings in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead a single program: every site scoped from a walkthrough in a shared format, inspected against the same QA rubric, reported side by side and managed by one account contact, with consolidated billing if you want it. It suits property managers, bank and credit union networks, healthcare groups, franchise operators, school systems and companies with several local offices or facilities.

## The multi-vendor problem

Organizations that grow site by site often end up with a different cleaner at each location. Each vendor has its own scope format, standard, invoice, insurance certificate and contact person. The result:

- No way to compare quality across sites
- Inconsistent experiences for customers, patients or tenants
- More vendors holding keys and codes to your buildings
- Administrative overhead: multiple contracts, certificates and invoices
- Problems handled differently at each site

Consolidating to one program solves most of that.

## How multi-site service works

### Each site scoped individually

Consolidation does not mean identical scopes. A branch with heavy foot traffic and a quiet back office need different tasks and frequencies. We walk each site and write its own scope, using a shared format so sites are easy to compare.

### One QA rubric

Supervisors inspect every site against the same rubric and report scores by site. You can see at a glance which building is performing and which needs attention. See our [quality assurance program](/quality-assurance-program) and [janitorial QA inspection scoring](/resources/janitorial-qa-inspection-scoring).

### One security standard

Every site follows the same credential logging, lock-up verification and escalation procedures, adjusted for each building's systems. Fewer vendors with access is also a security gain. See [security and access control](/security-and-access-control).

### One contact

Your account lead knows every site and owns issues across the portfolio. See [janitorial account management](/services/janitorial-account-management).

### Flexible billing

Consolidated invoicing across sites, invoices per site or per cost center, or invoices per owner entity for property managers. We set it up the way your accounting needs it.

## Standardization without rigidity

A common worry about consolidation is losing flexibility at individual sites. We keep both:

- **Standard:** scope format, QA rubric, security procedures, reporting, contact
- **Site-specific:** tasks, frequencies, schedules, restricted rooms, product preferences, local contacts

Site managers keep direct contact for day-to-day issues, while the portfolio lead sees everything.

## Rolling out across sites

We usually phase a multi-site transition:

1. **Pilot:** start with one or two representative sites and refine the scope format and reporting.
2. **Expand:** walk and scope remaining sites, coordinating start dates with current vendors' notice periods.
3. **Stabilize:** close QA in the first 60 days at each new site.
4. **Review:** compare site scores and adjust scopes after the first quarter.

Our guide to [switching janitorial companies](/resources/switching-janitorial-companies) covers handover at each site.

## What a portfolio report looks like

Each month, portfolio clients can see every site on one page: the latest supervisor score, the trend over the last few inspections, open deficiencies with correction dates, issues reported by site staff and how quickly they were closed, and any upcoming periodic work. Sites that slip below the agreed standard are flagged with the cause and the corrective plan. That single view replaces chasing several vendors for updates, and it gives regional managers and owners a factual basis for conversations about service levels and budgets.
