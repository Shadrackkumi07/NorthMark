Northmark Facility Services works as a local janitorial subcontractor for national facility management companies, integrated facility services providers and multi-site operators that need dependable service at locations in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead. We follow your scopes, use your reporting formats where required, carry our own insurance and bonding, screen every team member and provide local supervision that a distant prime contractor cannot.

## The problem national providers face in smaller metros

National facility management firms win contracts covering dozens or hundreds of sites: retail chains, bank branches, healthcare networks, corporate campuses. Their own crews are concentrated in large metros. When a portfolio includes a site in Fargo, Moorhead or West Fargo, they need a local partner who:

- Shows up reliably without daily oversight
- Follows the prime contractor's scope and standards exactly
- Reports in the format the prime contractor's client expects
- Handles compliance paperwork without chasing
- Escalates problems early, not after the client complains
- Is easy to reach and responsive

Finding that partner remotely is hard. Many local cleaners are set up for direct clients and struggle with the documentation, work-order systems and service-level reporting national contracts require.

## How Northmark works as a subcontractor

### Your scope, our execution

We adopt the scope of work you have contracted with your client. If your client's standards are stricter than ours, yours win. Where your scope is ambiguous, we ask before we interpret.

### Your systems where required

Many prime contractors use work-order platforms, check-in and check-out apps or inspection tools. We work within the systems you require, including logging visits, closing work orders and uploading inspection photos.

### Local supervision

Our Fargo–Moorhead supervisors inspect sites on a recurring schedule through our [quality assurance program](/quality-assurance-program). You get local eyes on your client's site without flying someone in.

### Compliance ready

- Northmark is [insured and bonded](/insured-and-bonded), with certificates issued to the prime contractor and client as required
- Every team member is [background-checked](/background-checked-staff) before receiving site access
- We complete client-specific badging, training or onboarding requirements before starting

### Clear escalation

Problems are reported to your designated contact quickly: access issues, damaged equipment, safety hazards or anything that could affect your client relationship. You hear it from us first.

## What we need from a prime contractor

A successful subcontract relationship is clear on both sides. We ask for:

1. The client scope of work and any service-level expectations
2. Site access procedures and contacts
3. Reporting and work-order requirements
4. Insurance and compliance requirements, including additional insured wording
5. Payment terms and invoicing format
6. Escalation contacts for both routine and urgent issues

Our guide to [janitorial contract terms](/resources/janitorial-contract-terms) covers clauses worth getting right in any subcontract.

## Why a local subcontractor beats a stretched national crew

Sending crews from Minneapolis or Sioux Falls to cover a Fargo site is expensive and fragile. Travel eats margin, weather cancels trips, and nobody knows the building. A local subcontractor with real supervision gives the prime contractor:

- Lower travel cost and fewer missed visits
- A team that learns the site
- Faster response to client requests
- Local knowledge of winter conditions, access quirks and building stock
