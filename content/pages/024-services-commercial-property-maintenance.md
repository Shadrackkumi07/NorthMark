Commercial property maintenance is the routine upkeep that keeps a building in good working order between major repairs and capital projects. Northmark provides commercial property maintenance for owners and property managers in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead: scheduled condition walkthroughs, general facility upkeep, and written reporting that tells you what was found, what was done and what needs a specialist. It runs on the same written scope, background-checked staff and supervisor oversight as our janitorial work.

## What commercial property maintenance covers

Property maintenance sits between cleaning and construction. It is not a remodel and it is not a roof replacement. It is the steady, scheduled attention that stops small problems from becoming large ones. At Northmark, a property maintenance scope is built for each building and typically draws from:

- **Scheduled condition walkthroughs** of common areas, restrooms, entries, mechanical spaces you designate, stairwells and exteriors
- **General upkeep tasks** agreed in the scope, such as replacing failed lamps in reachable fixtures, tightening loose hardware, adjusting doors that drag, replacing worn entry mats and touching up minor wear
- **Issue reporting** with photos and locations for anything outside the agreed upkeep scope
- **Seasonal checks** timed to Fargo–Moorhead's climate: freeze-thaw damage, roof drain and gutter observations, entry and sidewalk conditions, spring water intrusion
- **Documentation** that gives owners a running record of building condition

Work that requires a licensed trade, such as electrical circuits, plumbing repairs, HVAC service, fire and life safety systems, or roofing, is identified and reported to you for your contractors. We do not perform it ourselves, and we do not hide subcontracted trade work inside a maintenance invoice.

## Why routine maintenance pays off

Deferred maintenance is expensive. A slow roof drain becomes a ceiling leak. A failing door closer becomes a security gap. A loose stair nosing becomes a liability claim. Most of these failures give warning signs weeks or months in advance, but only if somebody is looking.

That is especially true in this climate. Fargo–Moorhead buildings go through long freezing winters, rapid spring thaws and summer severe weather, including hail. The National Weather Service office in Grand Forks issues regular warnings for the region on all three. Buildings that are walked and documented on a schedule show problems early. Buildings that are only inspected after a tenant complains show them late.

## Our property maintenance process

1. **Baseline walkthrough.** We walk the property, document its current condition and agree which upkeep tasks are in scope and which items go to your trades.
2. **Written scope and schedule.** Upkeep tasks, walkthrough frequency and reporting format are written down.
3. **Scheduled visits.** Upkeep and condition checks are completed on schedule against a checklist.
4. **Reports.** You receive findings with photos, actions taken and recommended follow-up. See [property condition reports](/services/property-condition-reports).
5. **Supervisor review.** A supervisor reviews completed work and open items as part of our [quality assurance program](/quality-assurance-program).

For deeper, recurring inspections, see [preventive maintenance walkthroughs](/services/preventive-maintenance-walkthroughs).

## Frequently asked questions

### What is commercial property maintenance?

Commercial property maintenance is the routine, scheduled upkeep of a commercial building: condition walkthroughs, general upkeep tasks and reporting that keep the property in good order between major repairs.

### Do you perform electrical, plumbing or HVAC work?

No. Work that requires a licensed trade is identified and reported to you so your contractors can handle it.

### Can maintenance be combined with janitorial service?

Yes. Many clients combine janitorial service and property maintenance under one scope and one account contact.

### How often are maintenance walkthroughs performed?

Walkthrough frequency is set in your scope, commonly monthly or quarterly, with seasonal checks added for winter and spring thaw.
