Facility maintenance is the work of keeping a business's building and its everyday systems functional, safe and presentable so operations are never interrupted by the building itself. Northmark provides facility maintenance for businesses in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead that do not have, or do not want, in-house maintenance staff: scheduled upkeep, issue tracking and written reporting, delivered by background-checked teams under one account contact.

## Facility maintenance vs property maintenance

The two overlap, but they are usually bought by different people:

- **Property maintenance** is typically bought by an owner or property manager and focuses on the asset: common areas, building envelope, long-term condition. See [commercial property maintenance](/services/commercial-property-maintenance).
- **Facility maintenance** is typically bought by the business that occupies the building, often an operations manager or office manager, and focuses on keeping the workplace running for employees and customers.

A distribution center's operations lead does not care about the owner's capital plan. They care that the breakroom works, the restroom fixtures function, the dock office door locks, and nothing in the building slows down a shift. Our guide to [facility maintenance vs facility management](/resources/facility-maintenance-vs-facility-management) explains where each discipline starts and stops.

## What we handle

Every facility maintenance scope is written for the building. Common elements include:

- **Workplace upkeep:** replacing failed lamps in reachable fixtures, adjusting and tightening doors, hardware, partitions and furniture, replacing worn mats, and handling minor wear and touch-ups
- **Restroom and breakroom function:** reporting and tracking fixture problems, supply dispensers and appliance issues
- **Egress and safety observations:** noting blocked exits, failed exit signs, missing fire extinguisher tags or damaged stair treads so your safety lead can act. OSHA's exit route standard requires exit routes to be kept free of obstructions, and its walking-working surfaces standard covers floor conditions.
- **Seasonal readiness:** entry matting and winter traffic plans, spring water checks, summer exterior observations
- **Issue log:** a running record of every reported problem, its status and who owns it
- **Vendor handoff:** clear, documented reports for items that need a licensed trade, so your electrician, plumber or HVAC contractor arrives knowing exactly what and where

## Why outsourced facility maintenance works for mid-size businesses

Large organizations employ facility engineers. Small ones call a handyman when something breaks. Mid-size businesses, the 20 to 300-employee companies that make up much of the Fargo–Moorhead economy, often fall between the two. Maintenance requests go to an office manager who already has a full job, and small issues pile up until they cause a problem.

Outsourced facility maintenance gives those businesses:

1. A **schedule**, so the building is checked even when nobody complains
2. A **log**, so issues do not get lost in email
3. A **single contact** who owns the issue until it is resolved or properly handed to a trade
4. **Predictable cost**, instead of a stream of ad hoc service calls

## Frequently asked questions

### What is included in facility maintenance?

Facility maintenance at Northmark includes scheduled workplace upkeep, safety and egress observations, seasonal readiness checks, an issue log and documented handoffs to licensed trades for work outside our scope.

### Who buys facility maintenance?

It is usually bought by the business occupying a building, often an operations or office manager, while property maintenance is usually bought by owners and property managers.

### Do you replace our in-house maintenance staff?

For many mid-size businesses, yes. Larger organizations sometimes use Northmark to support in-house staff with routine upkeep and documentation.

### Can you respond to urgent issues after hours?

After-hours response is part of how we operate. Urgent issues are handled through the escalation procedure agreed during onboarding.
