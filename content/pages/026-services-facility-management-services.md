Northmark offers facility management support for businesses and property owners in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead that need one accountable partner coordinating the routine care of a building: janitorial service, general upkeep, condition walkthroughs and reports, aerial documentation, and clear handoffs to licensed trades. It is designed for organizations that do not employ a full-time facility manager but still need the building run deliberately rather than reactively.

## What facility management means

The International Facility Management Association describes facility management as a profession that brings together people, place, process and technology so the built environment supports an organization. In large organizations that means a facility manager and a team covering maintenance, cleaning, security, space planning, energy, vendors and capital projects. The Bureau of Labor Statistics groups facilities managers with administrative services managers, who plan and coordinate the supportive services organizations need.

Most mid-size Fargo–Moorhead businesses do not have that person. Facility responsibilities land on an office manager, an operations lead or an owner who already has a full job. Requests pile up in email, vendors are managed ad hoc and problems are found when they break.

## What facility management support looks like day to day

### One issue log for the building

Every request, whether a cleaning miss, a failed light, a dripping faucet or a damaged door, goes into one log. Items we handle are scheduled and closed. Items needing a trade are documented with location, photos and symptoms and passed to your contractor. You see status in one place.

### Scheduled attention

Cleaning happens on schedule. Upkeep and condition walkthroughs happen on schedule. Seasonal checks for Fargo winters, spring thaw and summer storms are on the calendar before they are needed.

### Documentation that builds over time

Condition reports and aerial imagery create a dated record of the building. That record supports budget requests, insurance claims, lease negotiations and sale due diligence.

### A single accountable contact

Your account lead owns the building's routine care across services. See [janitorial account management](/services/janitorial-account-management).

## Facility maintenance vs facility management

The terms are often confused. Facility maintenance is hands-on upkeep. Facility management is the coordination of everything a building needs. Our guide to [facility maintenance vs facility management](/resources/facility-maintenance-vs-facility-management) explains the difference and which you need.
