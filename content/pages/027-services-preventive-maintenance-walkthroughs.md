A preventive maintenance walkthrough is a scheduled, checklist-driven inspection of a commercial building designed to catch developing problems early, such as slow leaks, failing hardware, blocked drains, worn sealants and egress hazards, before they cause damage, downtime or complaints. Northmark performs preventive maintenance walkthroughs for commercial properties in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead, with seasonal checklists built for the Red River Valley climate and a photo report after every visit.

## Why preventive beats reactive

The U.S. Department of Energy's operations and maintenance guidance for federal facilities compares reactive, preventive and predictive maintenance and explains why planned approaches generally outperform run-to-failure for many building components. The principle applies to any commercial building: a problem caught early is cheaper, safer and less disruptive than one discovered when it fails.

Consider how common building failures develop:

| Failure | Early warning | Cost if missed |
|---|---|---|
| Ceiling leak | Stain, sagging tile, blocked roof drain | Damaged insulation, finishes, inventory |
| Door security gap | Closer slamming or not latching | Unsecured building overnight |
| Slip injury | Worn mat, loose stair nosing, puddling | Injury and liability claim |
| Frozen pipe | Cold spot, drafty wall, failed heat in a vestibule | Water damage and downtime |
| Flooded lower level | Downspout discharging at the foundation, cracked wall | Major water damage in spring |
| Exit route blockage | Storage creeping into corridors | Code and safety exposure |

Each early warning is visible to someone walking the building with a checklist. Few are visible to busy occupants.

## What a walkthrough covers

Every checklist is written for the building, but typical items include:

**Interior**

- Ceilings, walls and floors for stains, cracks, damage and water signs
- Doors, closers, hardware and latching, especially exterior and fire-rated doors
- Restrooms: fixtures, leaks, dispensers, ventilation
- Lighting: failed lamps and fixtures in common areas
- Egress: exit signs lit, exit routes clear, which OSHA's exit route standard requires to be kept free of obstructions
- Mechanical and electrical rooms you designate: visible leaks, unusual sounds, storage hazards

**Exterior**

- Entries, sidewalks and steps for trip hazards and drainage
- Downspouts, splash blocks and grading
- Visible roof edges, gutters and drains from the ground
- Sealants, windows and visible facade condition
- Exterior lighting and signage
- Paving and curbs for cracks and potholes

**Seasonal additions**

- **Late fall:** weatherstripping, door sweeps, entry mats, drains clear before freeze
- **Winter:** ice formation points, entry conditions, cold spots, interior leaks from ice damming
- **Spring:** lower-level water, foundation and grading, winter paving damage, roof drainage after melt
- **Summer:** post-storm checks, sealants, landscaping against buildings

## What happens after the walkthrough

You receive a photo report organized by priority:

1. **Urgent:** safety, security or active damage, reported to you immediately rather than waiting for the written report
2. **Schedule with a trade:** items needing your licensed contractors, documented with location and photos
3. **Handled in scope:** upkeep tasks we completed during the visit
4. **Watch list:** items to recheck next visit

See [property condition reports](/services/property-condition-reports) for the report format.

## Frequently asked questions

### What is a preventive maintenance walkthrough?

A scheduled, checklist-based inspection of a building to find developing problems, such as leaks, hardware failures, drainage issues and safety hazards, before they cause damage.

### How often should a commercial building be inspected?

Monthly or quarterly is common, with seasonal checks added in late fall and spring for Fargo–Moorhead buildings.

### Do you fix what you find?

We handle upkeep tasks within the agreed scope and document anything requiring a licensed trade for your contractors.

### Will you tell us about urgent problems right away?

Yes. Safety, security and active damage findings are reported immediately, not held for the written report.
