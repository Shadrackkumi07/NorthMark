Northmark documents construction projects in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead with repeat drone flights from consistent vantage points, typically weekly, biweekly or monthly. Owners, developers, lenders and contractors receive dated imagery that shows progress across the whole site, supports draw requests and owner reports, and creates a permanent visual record of what was built and when. Flights are planned around active work and the site's safety rules.

## Why aerial progress documentation is valuable

Construction moves fast and covers a lot of ground. Ground-level photos capture details but rarely show the whole picture, and different people take them from different spots on different days, which makes comparison hard. Aerial documentation solves that:

- **Consistency:** the same angles and heights each visit, so changes are obvious
- **Coverage:** the entire site in a few images, including laydown areas, utilities and paving
- **Record:** a dated archive that is useful long after the project ends, for example to see where utilities run under a finished parking lot

Fargo–Moorhead keeps building. Fargo's city permit reporting showed 61 new commercial building permits valued at about $196 million for January–September 2025, and West Fargo continues to add commercial space. Every one of those projects has owners, lenders and partners who want to see progress.

- **Municipal and community stakeholders** following public-facing projects, with owner permission

## Safety on active sites

Construction sites are busy and hazardous. OSHA's construction resources cover the hazards contractors manage every day. Our drone work fits within the general contractor's safety program:

- We coordinate each visit with the site superintendent
- We follow site check-in, PPE and orientation requirements for any ground presence
- Flight paths avoid flying over workers where Part 107 rules restrict it, and avoid cranes and lifting operations
- We obtain FAA authorization where the site is in controlled airspace
- We pause or reschedule if conditions, activity or weather make flight unsafe

## Scheduling a progress program

Most projects use one of these cadences:

- **Weekly** for fast-moving or high-visibility projects
- **Biweekly** for typical commercial builds
- **Monthly**, aligned to draw schedules or owner meetings
- **Milestone-based**, at groundbreaking, foundation, topping out, enclosure, paving and completion

We set fixed vantage points in the first visit and use them for the life of the project.

## What this service is not

Northmark's construction work is documentation only. We do not provide post-construction cleanup, final cleaning or construction labor. We do not measure volumes, certify quantities or perform surveying that requires a licensed land surveyor.

## Why fixed vantage points matter

Progress imagery is only comparable if it is taken from the same place, height and angle each time. On the first visit we record each vantage point's position and altitude and reuse them throughout the project, adjusting only when cranes, new structures or airspace limits require it. The result is a set of images that can be placed side by side or turned into a simple time-lapse for owner meetings, so stakeholders can see a whole season of work in a few seconds rather than scrolling through hundreds of unrelated photos.

## Frequently asked questions

### How often should construction progress be documented?

Biweekly or monthly is common for commercial projects, with weekly flights for fast or high-visibility builds and milestone flights at key stages.

### Do you coordinate with the general contractor?

Yes. Each visit is coordinated with the site superintendent and follows the site's safety requirements.

### Do you provide post-construction cleaning?

No. Northmark provides aerial documentation during construction and recurring janitorial service after occupancy, but not post-construction cleanup.

### Can lenders use the imagery for draws?

Lenders often use dated aerial imagery to review progress. Each lender sets its own requirements.
