Northmark provides janitorial service to outpatient healthcare facilities in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead: multi-specialty clinics, physical and occupational therapy centers, behavioral health and counseling offices, urgent care and walk-in clinics, imaging centers, medical office buildings and healthcare administrative buildings. We clean patient-facing, staff and administrative areas with background-checked, privacy-trained staff, agree clinical boundaries in writing and verify work through supervisor QA.

## Healthcare is a major part of the local economy

Fargo–Moorhead is a regional healthcare center. Bureau of Labor Statistics data show healthcare practitioner and technical occupations at 8.5% of metro employment, compared with 6.3% nationally. Census data show about $4.16 billion in 2022 health care and social assistance revenue in Cass County and about $429 million in Clay County. Alongside the major health systems, the metro has a dense network of outpatient facilities, many in buildings that need dependable janitorial service.

## How we approach healthcare facilities

### Agreed clinical boundaries

Before service begins, we agree in writing which surfaces our team cleans and which clinical staff clean. Exam tables, treatment equipment, therapy equipment, imaging equipment, sharps and regulated waste stay with clinical staff and licensed haulers.

### Risk-based attention

The CDC's environmental infection control guidelines stress routine cleaning of frequently touched surfaces in patient areas. Our scopes concentrate effort on door handles, check-in counters, waiting room seating arms, restroom fixtures, elevator buttons and handrails, the surfaces many hands touch.

### Approved products

Products used in patient areas are agreed with each facility's infection prevention or practice lead, so they are compatible with surfaces and protocols. Our guide to [cleaning vs sanitizing vs disinfecting](/resources/cleaning-vs-sanitizing-vs-disinfecting) explains the terms.

### Privacy

Staff are background-checked, assigned to each facility and trained not to read, move or photograph records, screens or patient belongings. Behavioral health facilities in particular need cleaning staff who understand discretion.

### Supervisor QA

Recurring inspections score the patient path, from entry to check-in, waiting, restrooms and exam or treatment areas, against the scope. See our [quality assurance program](/quality-assurance-program).

## Extended and irregular hours

Urgent care and walk-in clinics open early, close late and run weekends. Therapy centers may have early-morning and evening sessions. We schedule cleaning around actual operating hours, which can mean late-night, early-morning or split visits.

## Healthcare groups with several locations

Many healthcare organizations operate several outpatient sites across Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead. We serve all three cities with one scope format, one QA rubric and one account contact, with results reported by site. See [multi-site janitorial](/services/multi-site-janitorial).

## Winter and patient safety

Many outpatient patients are elderly, injured or mobility-limited. Wet, salty entries in a Fargo–Moorhead winter are a fall risk. Healthcare scopes include extra entry and floor attention from November through April, with prompt reporting of matting or drainage problems.

## Behavioral health and therapy settings

Behavioral health and counseling offices need a particular kind of care. Soft furnishings, rugs and comfortable seating are common, and the atmosphere matters as much as the surfaces. Our teams vacuum upholstered seating as scoped, keep waiting rooms calm and tidy, and never disturb client files or personal items in therapists' offices. In physical and occupational therapy clinics, open gym floors take heavy traffic, so floor care and entry control get extra attention, while mats, tables and exercise equipment remain with clinicians who clean them between patients.

## Frequently asked questions

### What types of healthcare facilities do you clean?

Clinics, therapy centers, behavioral health offices, urgent care, imaging centers, medical office buildings and healthcare administration buildings across Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead.

### Do you clean therapy equipment or exam tables?

No. Clinical equipment, exam tables and therapy equipment are cleaned by clinical staff. We clean floors, restrooms, waiting areas, offices and designated surfaces.

### Can you clean after evening clinic hours?

Yes. We schedule around actual operating hours, including late evenings, early mornings and weekends.

### Can one contract cover several clinic locations?

Yes. We serve multiple sites under one program with reporting by site.
