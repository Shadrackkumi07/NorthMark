## Why financial institutions are a natural fit for security-first cleaning

Banks and credit unions run some of the most security-conscious buildings in any community. Federal rules such as 12 CFR Part 21 require national banks to designate a security officer and maintain a security program with procedures for opening and closing, safekeeping cash and other valuables, and related controls. Credit unions and state-chartered institutions operate under comparable expectations. Examiners and auditors look at vendor access as part of that program.

Yet the janitorial vendor is often the only outside party with regular, after-hours access to a branch. A vendor without disciplined procedures is a gap auditors notice. Northmark's operating model was built around closing that gap, which is why financial institutions are one of our core markets.

## How we clean a branch securely

### People

- Every team member is background-checked before receiving access ([background-checked staff](/background-checked-staff))
- Staff are assigned to specific branches; changes are communicated in advance
- Institutions with additional screening requirements, such as fingerprint checks or their own vendor vetting, can require them before assignment

### Credentials

- Keys and fobs tagged with codes, not branch names or addresses
- Credentials logged to individuals, with issue and return dates
- Individual alarm codes where the system supports it, so every arm and disarm event is traceable
- Lost credentials reported to your security officer immediately

### Procedures

- Entry and exit timed to your opening and closing procedures
- Cash areas, vaults, teller drawers, safe deposit areas and server rooms excluded unless your security officer specifies otherwise
- Desks with documents left in place, and no handling of papers, screens or devices
- Lock-up checklist completed every visit, with failures escalated by your procedure

Our [security and access control](/security-and-access-control) page explains these standards in detail, and [vendor key and access control](/resources/vendor-key-and-access-control) gives your security officer a vendor-neutral checklist.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do your cleaners enter the vault or cash areas?

No, unless your security officer specifically authorizes defined tasks under defined conditions. By default those areas are excluded.

### Can we require fingerprint background checks?

Yes. If your institution requires additional screening, we complete it before assigning staff to your branches.

### How are alarm codes handled?
