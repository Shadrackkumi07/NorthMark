Northmark provides janitorial service and commercial property maintenance to property management companies in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead. Each building gets its own written scope, certificates of insurance are issued per property, supervisors score every site on a recurring schedule, and one account contact handles your whole portfolio. The result is consistent standards across buildings and reporting you can pass to owners.

## What property managers need from a cleaning vendor

A property manager is not buying cleaning for themselves. They are buying it on behalf of owners and for the benefit of tenants, and they answer to both. That changes what matters:

- **Tenant satisfaction.** Common-area cleanliness is one of the most visible signs of how a building is run. Restroom and lobby complaints reach the manager first.
- **Owner reporting.** Owners want evidence that the money they spend on services is producing results.
- **Compliance.** Every vendor needs current insurance certificates naming the right entities, often one per property.
- **Consistency.** A manager with eight buildings cannot manage eight different standards and eight vendors.
- **Responsiveness.** When a tenant reports a problem, the manager needs a vendor who fixes it the same day and confirms it.

Northmark's operating model was designed around these needs.

## How we serve property management portfolios

### One scope format, many buildings

Each building has its own scope, built from a walkthrough, but every scope uses the same format and the same QA rubric. That lets you compare buildings side by side and spot the one that needs attention. See [multi-site janitorial](/services/multi-site-janitorial).

### Certificates per property

We issue certificates of insurance per property and per owner entity, with additional insured wording where your management agreement requires it. Northmark is [insured and bonded](/insured-and-bonded).

### Site-level QA reporting

Supervisors walk each building on a recurring schedule and score it against its scope. You receive results by site, including open items and correction dates. See our [quality assurance program](/quality-assurance-program).

### Tenant issue handling

When a tenant reports a problem, you send it to your Northmark contact once. We log it, fix it and confirm with you. Repeated issues trigger a scope or staffing review rather than repeated apologies.

### Cleaning and upkeep together

Many managers combine janitorial service with [commercial property maintenance](/services/commercial-property-maintenance): condition walkthroughs, general upkeep and reports on items that need your trades. Our cleaning teams log issues they see during visits, so problems reach you earlier.
