Northmark provides janitorial service for manufacturing plants in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead. We clean offices, locker rooms, restrooms, breakrooms, lunchrooms, training rooms, quality labs the plant designates and shared corridors, and sweep designated walkways. Work is scheduled around shifts and done under your safety program. Production lines, machinery and anything under lockout stay with your trained staff.

## Manufacturing in Fargo–Moorhead

Manufacturing is one of the region's important industries. Greater Fargo Moorhead Economic Development lists it alongside distribution, healthcare, AgTech and technology. Local plants produce agricultural equipment and components, food products, building materials, metal fabrication and more. Many run multiple shifts with large workforces relying on shared locker rooms, restrooms and lunchrooms.

That workforce is the reason janitorial service matters in a plant. Production equipment gets cleaned by operators and maintenance staff who know it. But the spaces where people change, eat, wash and rest need a dedicated team, and those spaces strongly shape morale and retention.

## What we clean in a plant

| Area | Why it matters | Typical frequency |
|---|---|---|
| Locker rooms and showers | Used by every shift; hygiene and morale | After each shift change or daily |
| Restrooms (plant and office) | OSHA 1910.141 sanitation requirements; heavy use | Daily or more |
| Lunchrooms and breakrooms | Food areas next to production dust | Daily, between meal periods |
| Offices, HR and front office | Administrative function and visitor impressions | Nightly or several times a week |
| Training and conference rooms | Onboarding, safety meetings, customer visits | As scheduled |
| Designated walkways and aisles | Safe movement and housekeeping support | As scoped |
| Entrances and stairs | Tracked debris spreads quickly | Daily |

OSHA's sanitation standard sets requirements for toilet facilities, washing facilities and, where employees eat on site, the condition of eating areas. Clean, stocked, working facilities are one of the most direct things a janitorial partner contributes to compliance and to how employees feel about their workplace.

## Production boundaries

The most important part of a plant scope is what it excludes. By default:

- **No production equipment.** Machines, conveyors, process lines and tooling are cleaned by plant staff.
- **No lockout areas.** OSHA's hazardous energy standard governs servicing and maintenance. Our team never touches equipment under lockout and stays clear of servicing work.
- **No hazardous process cleanup.** Chemical spills, process residue and specialized sanitation are outside our scope.
- **Restricted zones honored.** Clean rooms, labs, chemical storage and high-hazard areas are excluded unless your safety team designates specific tasks.

## Safety rules we follow

- Your PPE requirements (safety glasses, hearing protection, safety shoes, high-visibility vests)
- Marked pedestrian walkways and forklift traffic rules
- Site safety orientation before our team starts
- Hazard communication for our products, with safety data sheets available, and awareness of your chemical hazards
- Immediate reporting of spills, leaks or hazards to your designated contact

## Shift-based scheduling

Plants rarely have a quiet window. We schedule zone by zone:

- **Locker rooms and restrooms:** after shift changes, when they need it most
- **Lunchrooms:** between meal periods
- **Offices:** after administrative hours
- **Walkways:** during the lowest-traffic window you designate

Schedules are written into the scope and checked during supervisor QA walkthroughs.

No. Production equipment is cleaned by your trained staff. We clean locker rooms, restrooms, lunchrooms, offices, training rooms and designated walkways.

### Can you clean locker rooms between shifts?

Yes. Locker rooms and restrooms are usually scheduled right after shift changes.

### Will your team complete our safety orientation?

Yes. Our team completes your site safety orientation and follows your PPE and walkway rules.

### Do you follow our photography and confidentiality rules?

Yes. Staff are trained on your restricted areas and photography rules during onboarding.
