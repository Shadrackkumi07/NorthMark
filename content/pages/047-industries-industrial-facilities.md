Northmark provides janitorial service for industrial facilities in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead: fabrication shops, processing facilities, contractors' yards and shops, light industrial buildings and similar sites. We clean offices, locker rooms, restrooms, breakrooms, training rooms and shared corridors, and sweep accessible floor areas the site designates. We work around production schedules and under your safety program, with background-checked staff, written scopes and supervisor QA. We do not clean production equipment or anything under lockout.

## Industrial cleaning means different things to different people

Search "industrial cleaning services" and you will find everything from hydro-excavation and tank cleaning to office janitorial. These are very different services with different equipment, training and risks. To be clear about where Northmark fits:

- **What we do:** routine janitorial work in the employee, administrative and shared areas of industrial buildings, plus sweeping or dust-mopping of accessible floor areas.
- **What we do not do:** heavy industrial cleaning such as tank cleaning, hydro-blasting, process equipment cleaning, hazardous material cleanup, confined space work or work on equipment under lockout.

That clarity matters. A janitorial vendor who drifts into production areas without the right training creates risk for everyone. When industrial clients need specialty cleaning, they should hire a specialty contractor, and we will say so.

## What we clean at an industrial site

| Area | Why it matters | Typical scope |
|---|---|---|
| Locker rooms and showers | Heavy daily use by shift crews | Fixtures, benches, floors, waste, supply reporting |
| Restrooms | Required sanitation facilities under OSHA 1910.141 | Fixtures, partitions, floors, dispensers |
| Breakrooms and lunchrooms | Food areas next to dusty work areas | Tables, counters, sinks, appliance exteriors, floors |
| Offices and training rooms | Administrative function and visitor impressions | Trash, dusting, vacuuming, hard floors |
| Corridors, stairs and entries | Tracked-in debris spreads quickly | Floors, handrails, mats, glass |
| Designated floor areas | Walkways and aisles that support safe movement | Sweeping or dust-mopping as scoped |

OSHA's sanitation standard sets requirements for toilet facilities, washing facilities and, where food is consumed, eating areas. Keeping those spaces clean is one of the most practical things a janitorial vendor does for an industrial employer.

## Safety rules we follow

Industrial sites have hazards we take seriously:

- **Lockout/tagout:** OSHA's hazardous energy standard protects workers servicing equipment. Our teams never clean, touch or move equipment under lockout, and they stay out of areas where servicing is under way.
- **Hazard communication:** our team members are trained on the chemicals we bring, with safety data sheets available. We also need to know about hazardous materials in your areas we clean.
- **PPE and walkways:** we follow your PPE requirements and marked pedestrian routes.
- **Restricted areas:** production lines, labs, chemical storage and similar spaces are excluded unless your safety team specifically designates otherwise.
- **Incident reporting:** spills, leaks and hazards are reported to your designated contact right away.

## Scheduling around production

Industrial sites often run around the clock. We schedule each zone around its own use: offices after administrative hours, locker rooms and restrooms after shift changes, breakrooms between meal periods, floors when traffic is lowest. Your site's quiet windows go into the written scope.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you provide heavy industrial cleaning?

No. Northmark provides janitorial service for the employee, administrative and shared areas of industrial facilities. Tank cleaning, hydro-blasting, process equipment cleaning and hazardous material cleanup belong with specialty contractors.

### Will your team clean around our equipment?

Our team sweeps accessible floor areas the site designates and stays clear of operating equipment. We never touch equipment under lockout.

### Can you clean locker rooms between shifts?

Yes. Locker rooms and restrooms are commonly scheduled after shift changes, when they need attention most.

### Do you follow our safety program?

Yes. We follow your PPE rules, walkways, restricted areas and reporting procedures.
