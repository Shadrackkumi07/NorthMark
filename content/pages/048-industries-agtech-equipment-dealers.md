Northmark provides janitorial service for agricultural equipment dealers, implement and parts businesses, and AgTech companies in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead. For dealerships we zone the building into showroom, sales offices, parts counter, service write-up, customer lounge, restrooms and shop-side employee areas, each with its own tasks and schedule. For AgTech firms we combine office and lab-adjacent cleaning with confidentiality. Seasonal surges around planting and harvest are built into the scope.

## Agriculture and AgTech in Fargo–Moorhead

The Red River Valley is one of the most productive farming regions in the country, and USDA statistics show North Dakota among the leading producers of crops such as spring wheat, soybeans, sunflowers and dry beans. Fargo–Moorhead serves as the commercial hub for that agriculture, with implement dealers, parts suppliers and service businesses lining the region's highways. Greater Fargo Moorhead Economic Development also identifies AgTech as a target industry, with technology, software and research companies serving agriculture.

These businesses have buildings unlike typical offices, and their cleaning needs change with the farm calendar.

## Equipment dealerships: a zoned approach

| Zone | What matters | Typical scope |
|---|---|---|
| Showroom | First impression for customers making large purchases | Floors, glass, displays, seating, entry, cleaned before opening |
| Sales offices | Professional setting for negotiations | Trash, dusting, vacuuming, desks when cleared |

| Shop-side breakroom and locker area | Technicians' daily space | Tables, counters, sinks, floors, trash |

## Built for the farm calendar

Dealerships do not have a steady year. Spring planting and fall harvest bring rushes of parts customers and service work, long shop hours and heavy restroom and breakroom use. Winter is quieter for some dealers and busy with shop rebuilds for others.

We adjust:

- **Higher frequency in shop restrooms and breakrooms** during planting and harvest
- **More entry and floor attention** in muddy spring and dusty fall
- **Flexible timing** when the shop runs late, cleaning customer areas first and shop-side areas after technicians leave
- **Winter entry program** for snow, slush and ice melt from November through April

## AgTech companies

AgTech firms in the metro range from software startups to companies with labs, greenhouses and test equipment. Their buildings combine offices, collaboration space and technical areas. We clean offices, kitchens, restrooms and common areas, honor restricted labs and test areas, and keep strict confidentiality around prototypes, data and screens. ## Safety and site rules

Dealership shops and AgTech labs have hazards: equipment movement, lifts, chemicals, fluids and sharp parts. Our team follows your PPE and restricted-area rules, never touches equipment or tools, and reports spills or hazards. OSHA's sanitation standard applies to employee restrooms and washing facilities, which take the heaviest load at dealerships during peak seasons.

## Customer areas that sell equipment

Equipment purchases are large, and buyers often spend time in a dealership before committing. A clean customer lounge, a restroom that reflects well on the business and a showroom floor free of tracked-in mud all support the sale. We schedule customer areas to be finished before opening, and during busy seasons we can add a second visit later in the week so the lounge and restrooms do not fall behind. Parts counters, where farmers stop in during long working days, get the same attention.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you clean our showroom before we open?

Yes. Showrooms and customer areas can be cleaned early in the morning so they are spotless at opening.

### Do you clean service bays and equipment?

No. Bays, equipment and tools stay with your technicians. We clean customer areas, offices, restrooms, breakrooms and designated walkways.

### Can service increase during planting and harvest?

Yes. We increase frequency in restrooms, breakrooms and entries during peak seasons and scale back afterward.

### Do you serve AgTech offices and labs?

Yes. We clean offices and common areas, honor restricted labs and keep confidentiality around prototypes and data.
