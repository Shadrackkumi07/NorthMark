Northmark provides janitorial service for retail stores and storefront businesses in Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead: boutiques, specialty retailers, salons and studios, service counters, showrooms and multi-unit retail centers. We focus on what shoppers notice: entry glass, floors, checkout areas, fitting rooms and restrooms. Cleaning happens after close or early in the morning before opening, by background-checked staff following a written scope.

## Retail cleaning is about first impressions

Shoppers judge a store before they reach the first display. Smudged door glass, a gritty entry mat, dust on shelves or a neglected restroom all signal carelessness, and those signals carry over to how customers feel about the merchandise. Unlike an office, where employees tolerate a little dust, a store's cleanliness directly affects sales.

Retail floors also carry heavy traffic, and slips on wet entries are a real risk. OSHA's walking-working surfaces standard requires floors to be kept clean and, as far as practical, free of hazards, and wet, salty winter entries are where most slip risks begin.

## What we clean in a storefront

| Area | Typical scope |
|---|---|
| Entry and vestibule | Door glass inside and out within reach, door hardware, mats, entry floor |
| Sales floor | Vacuuming or mopping, dusting reachable fixtures and shelving edges, trash |
| Checkout and service counters | Counter surfaces, card terminals' exterior where approved, glass, floor |
| Fitting rooms | Mirrors, benches, hooks, floors, trash |
| Restrooms | Fixtures, mirrors, floors, waste, supply reporting |
| Stockroom and back office | Trash, floors, breakroom area |
| Display windows | Interior glass and sills as scoped |

The CDC notes that routine cleaning removes most germs from surfaces, with disinfection added for frequently touched surfaces in higher-risk settings. Checkout counters, door handles and fitting room hooks are the touchpoints we prioritize.

## Timing around store hours

Retail cleaning has to fit around customers:

- **After close:** the most common option, so the store is ready in the morning
- **Early morning before opening:** good for stores open late, so floors and glass are freshest at opening
- **Day porter visits:** not something we offer, but we can increase evening frequency for busy seasons

Holiday seasons, sales events and back-to-school periods bring more traffic. We increase frequency or add visits during those weeks.

## Winter in Fargo–Moorhead retail

From November through April, every shopper brings snow, slush and ice melt through the door. Entry mats saturate, salt residue dulls floors and sand gets ground into carpet. Retail scopes include a winter mode with more entry attention, mat management and floor cleaning focused on the first 20 feet inside the door. ## Fitting rooms and checkout lanes

Two retail areas deserve special mention. Fitting rooms collect hangers, tags, dust and fingerprints on mirrors, and customers notice immediately when they are neglected. Checkout lanes and counters see the most hands of any surface in the store. Our scopes treat both as every-visit priorities, with mirrors cleaned, benches wiped, floors vacuumed and counter surfaces cleaned with products approved for your fixtures. Card terminals and registers are handled only as you approve, so equipment is never damaged.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you clean our store before we open?

Yes. Early-morning cleaning before opening is common for stores that close late.

### Do you clean display windows?

We clean interior display glass and sills within reach as scoped. Exterior high windows are outside our scope.

### Can you increase service during the holidays?

Yes. We add visits or increase frequency during holiday seasons and sales events.

### Do your cleaners have access to cash areas?

No. Cash areas and safes are off-limits, and alarm and lock-up procedures are agreed in writing.
