## Why we focus on front of house

Restaurant kitchens are regulated food establishments. The FDA Food Code, adopted in some form by state and local health authorities, sets requirements for cleaning and sanitizing food-contact surfaces, equipment and utensils. In this market, North Dakota and Minnesota agencies oversee food establishment licensing and inspection. Kitchen sanitation is tightly tied to your food safety training, your sanitizer concentrations, your equipment and your inspection history, and it belongs with your own crew or a specialist kitchen cleaning contractor.

Front of house is different. Guests judge the dining room, entry and restrooms, and a restaurant crew finishing a late shift often does not have the time or energy to detail them properly. That is where a janitorial partner adds the most value.

## Restrooms make or break reviews

Restaurant reviews frequently mention restrooms. A guest who finds a dirty restroom often assumes the kitchen looks the same. Nightly detailed restroom cleaning, with fixtures, partitions, floors and supply reporting, is the single most important part of a restaurant janitorial scope.

## Floors in a restaurant

Dining room floors collect food debris, spills and grease tracked from the kitchen, and in winter, salt and slush from the entry. Regular sweeping and mopping with appropriate products keeps them safe and presentable. Our scope covers routine floor cleaning. Restorative work such as stripping and refinishing is outside our services.

## Timing

Restaurants close late and open for lunch or breakfast. Two windows work:

- **Late night after close:** our team arrives after your crew leaves
- **Early morning before opening:** floors and restrooms are freshest when the first guests arrive

We coordinate with your closing manager so our team knows when the building is clear and how to lock up.

## Coordinating the handoff each night

The cleanest restaurant programs rely on a simple nightly handoff between the closing crew and our team. Your crew finishes kitchen sanitation, stacks chairs if agreed, and confirms the building is clear. Our team arrives, cleans floors, restrooms and front-of-house areas, and leaves a short note if anything needs attention, such as a broken dispenser or a slow drain. That note goes to your manager before the next shift, so problems are fixed before guests notice them.
