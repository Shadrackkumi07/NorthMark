## What makes Fargo different to clean

### Winter is half the year

From roughly November through April, Fargo entrances take a beating from snow, slush, ice melt and sand. Salt residue dulls hard floors and gets ground into carpet, and wet entries become slip hazards. Fargo cleaning scopes need a winter mode with more frequent entry attention, mat management and floor checks. Our guide to [winter facility care in Fargo](/resources/winter-facility-care-fargo) covers the details.

### Spring thaw and the Red River

Spring brings meltwater, and in some years Red River flooding. Basements, lower levels and building envelopes show water problems first. Our maintenance teams watch for them, and our guide to [Red River flood season property prep](/resources/red-river-flood-season-property-prep) helps owners get ready.

### A growing city

Fargo issued 61 new commercial building permits valued at about $196 million for January–September 2025, according to city permit reporting. New buildings bring new tenants and new expectations, and owners who set clear cleaning standards from day one avoid problems later.
