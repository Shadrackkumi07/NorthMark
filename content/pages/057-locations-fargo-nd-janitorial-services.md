Northmark provides recurring janitorial services to commercial buildings throughout Fargo, North Dakota, including the 58102, 58103 and 58104 ZIP codes. ## What Fargo businesses tell us about janitorial service

When Fargo office managers, practice administrators and property managers explain why they are looking for a new janitorial provider, the reasons are remarkably consistent:

1. **Missed nights.** The contract says five nights, but some nights nobody comes, and nobody tells them.
2. **Crew turnover.** New people every few weeks who do not know the building.
3. **Nobody checks.** The first quality inspection happens when an employee complains.
4. **Slow response.** Calls and emails go to a general inbox.
5. **Unclear scope.** Arguments about whether something was "included."

Cleaning technique is rarely the issue. Management is. Our janitorial service is built to fix those five problems specifically.

## How Northmark runs janitorial service in Fargo

### Assigned teams, not rotating crews

Each Fargo building has an assigned team. Consistent assignment means the people cleaning your building know its layout, alarm system, problem areas and priorities. If an assignment changes, you are told who is coming.

### Visit confirmation

Arrival and departure are recorded for each visit, and checklist completion is logged. If something prevents a visit, such as a severe winter storm that closes roads, you hear about it from us first, along with when service will resume.

### Supervisor inspections

Supervisors walk each building on a schedule written into your scope and score it against the checklist. Deficiencies are logged with correction dates. See our [quality assurance program](/quality-assurance-program).

### Direct contact

Your account contact is a person in Fargo, not a call center. Report an issue once and it is owned until it is fixed and confirmed.
