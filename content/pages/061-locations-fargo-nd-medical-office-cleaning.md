Northmark provides janitorial service for medical and dental offices, specialty clinics, therapy practices and outpatient offices in Fargo, North Dakota. We clean waiting rooms, restrooms, corridors, offices, staff areas and practice-designated exam room surfaces after hours, with background-checked staff trained on patient privacy and a written agreement on what clinical staff handle and what we handle.

## Fargo's healthcare concentration

Fargo serves as a medical destination for a large part of North Dakota and western Minnesota. The data reflect it:

- **Employment share:** Bureau of Labor Statistics data show healthcare practitioner and technical occupations make up 8.5% of Fargo-area employment, compared with 6.3% nationally.
- **Revenue:** Census data show about $4.16 billion in 2022 health care and social assistance revenue in Cass County.
- **Footprint:** major health systems run large campuses in the city, surrounded by independent clinics, dental practices, chiropractors, physical therapy and behavioral health offices, imaging centers and specialty groups.

Many of those practices sit in south Fargo's medical and professional districts, but clinics are found in every part of the city, including downtown and along the main commercial corridors.

## How we work in Fargo clinics

### A written split with clinical staff

Before the first visit we agree, in writing, which surfaces our team cleans and which your clinical staff clean between patients. Exam tables, medical equipment, instruments, sharps containers and regulated waste stay with clinical staff and licensed haulers. That clarity prevents gaps.

### Products agreed with the practice

Disinfectants used in patient areas are agreed with your practice, so they fit your surfaces and your infection prevention program. The CDC's environmental infection control guidance emphasizes routine cleaning of frequently touched surfaces in patient areas, and we focus effort there.

### Privacy-trained, background-checked staff

Assigned team members are background-checked and trained not to read, move or photograph charts, documents or screens. Medication rooms and records areas are excluded or cleaned under your conditions. See [background-checked staff](/background-checked-staff).

### Supervisor QA

Supervisors inspect each clinic on a recurring schedule and score it against the scope. Waiting rooms and restrooms, the areas patients judge first, get particular attention.

## Fargo-specific factors

- **Winter entries.** Patients arriving with snow, salt and slush make clinic entrances and waiting rooms a winter priority, especially for practices serving elderly or mobility-limited patients for whom a wet floor is a fall risk.
- **Extended hours.** Many Fargo practices run early, evening or Saturday hours. We schedule around them.
- **Growth.** New clinics keep opening in south Fargo and along the I-29 and I-94 corridors. Setting a clear cleaning standard at opening is much easier than fixing one later.

## Supporting a good patient experience

Patients rarely compliment a clinic for being clean, but they notice immediately when it is not. Online reviews of Fargo practices regularly mention restrooms, waiting rooms and how a place smells. Our scopes therefore weight the patient path: entry, check-in, waiting area, restroom, corridor and exam room floor. Supervisors inspect along that same path during QA walkthroughs, because it is the route your patients take every day. Practices with several locations across Fargo, West Fargo and Moorhead can run them all under one program, with results reported by site, so a practice manager can see at a glance which location needs attention.

## Frequently asked questions

### Do you clean medical offices in south Fargo?

Yes. We serve clinics and practices throughout Fargo, including south Fargo's medical and professional districts.

### Do your staff handle medical waste?

No. Regulated medical waste and sharps are handled by the practice and its licensed waste hauler.

### Can you clean around our evening clinic hours?

Yes. We schedule cleaning after your last patient, including late evenings and weekends.

### Which disinfectants do you use?

Products used in patient areas are agreed with each practice so they match your surfaces and infection prevention protocols.
