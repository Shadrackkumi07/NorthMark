## West Fargo is growing, and its buildings are mixed

West Fargo had an estimated 41,175 residents in 2025, up 6.6% from its 2020 base, according to the U.S. Census Bureau. It is one of the fastest-growing cities in the state, and its commercial base reflects that growth: new office and retail development along its main corridors, schools and community facilities serving young families, and an industrial and distribution base with direct access to I-94.

What stands out about West Fargo is how many commercial buildings combine several uses under one roof:

- **Contractor and trade buildings** with offices up front and a shop or yard behind
- **Equipment and agricultural dealers** with a showroom, service bays and parts storage
- **Distribution and light industrial buildings** with office wings, driver areas and warehouse floors
- **New professional buildings** with clinics, financial offices and service businesses sharing common areas

A one-size office checklist does not work for these buildings. We use a zoned approach, described on our [facility cleaning](/services/facility-cleaning) page, with each zone getting its own tasks and schedule.

## What West Fargo buildings need from a cleaning vendor

### Newer buildings, higher expectations

Much of West Fargo's commercial space is relatively new. Owners of new buildings want to protect finishes and set a standard from day one. A written scope and supervisor QA help keep a new building looking new.

### Dust from the shop side

In mixed buildings, dust and debris from shop and warehouse areas spread into offices and restrooms. Scopes for these buildings put extra attention on entries, transition zones and HVAC returns we can reach.

### Winter on open sites

West Fargo's open, wind-exposed sites mean drifting snow and heavy ice-melt use at entrances. Entry matting and floor care get a winter mode from November to April. See [winter facility care in Fargo](/resources/winter-facility-care-fargo), which applies equally to West Fargo.
