Northmark provides recurring janitorial service to commercial buildings in West Fargo, North Dakota: professional and medical offices, bank and credit union branches, schools and childcare centers, retail and service businesses, and the office areas of West Fargo's industrial and distribution buildings. ## A young, fast-growing commercial base

West Fargo grew to an estimated 41,175 residents in 2025, up 6.6% from its 2020 base. Much of that growth came with new neighborhoods on the south and west sides, and commercial development followed. As a result, a large share of West Fargo's commercial buildings are relatively new:

- Medical and dental clinics serving young families
- Professional offices and financial branches along the main corridors
- Childcare centers, learning centers and youth activity facilities
- Restaurants, fitness centers and service businesses in new retail strips
- Offices attached to warehouses, contractors and equipment businesses

New buildings have their own janitorial priorities. Owners want to protect new finishes, set a standard from the first week and avoid the slow slide that happens when nobody checks.
