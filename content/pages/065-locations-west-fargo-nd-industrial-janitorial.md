Northmark provides janitorial service for West Fargo's industrial buildings: warehouses and distribution facilities, contractor shops and yards, equipment and agricultural dealers, fabrication shops and light manufacturing. We clean offices, dispatch areas, restrooms, locker rooms, breakrooms and designated floor areas around your crews and under your safety rules, with background-checked staff and supervisor QA.

## West Fargo's industrial character

West Fargo has long been the metro's industrial side. Its history includes stockyards and rail, and today its industrial areas host trucking and distribution, construction contractors, equipment dealers, agricultural service businesses, fabrication shops and light manufacturers. Many buildings sit on large lots with yards, wash bays and equipment storage, close to I-94.

These buildings share a pattern: a front office and dispatch area, restrooms and a breakroom used by both office staff and crews, and a large shop, warehouse or service area. That pattern drives the janitorial approach.

## Mud, dust and snow: three seasons of debris

West Fargo industrial sites deal with heavy debris most of the year:

- **Spring:** thaw turns yards and gravel lots to mud, and crews track it into every entry.
- **Summer and harvest:** dry conditions and field equipment bring dust and chaff into shops and offices.
- **Winter:** snow, slush and ice melt come in on boots and equipment.

Scopes for these buildings put extra attention on entries, mudrooms, transition areas and restrooms near the shop. OSHA's walking-working surfaces standard calls for floors to be kept clean and as free of hazards as practical, and entry floors are where slips most often begin.

## Working around crews

Crews in West Fargo shops start early and leave mid-afternoon, or run split shifts in busy seasons. We schedule:

- **Locker rooms and restrooms** after crews leave or between shifts
- **Offices** after administrative hours
- **Shop walkways** when the shop is quiet
- **Customer areas** before opening, for dealerships and parts counters

We follow your PPE rules and site safety orientation, stay out of active work areas and never touch equipment or tools.

## Inspections built for working buildings

Supervisor QA in an industrial building looks different from an office inspection. Supervisors check restrooms and locker rooms at the times crews actually use them, confirm entry and mudroom floors are safe during wet seasons, and verify that our team is following your walkway and PPE rules. Findings are reported in plain language with photos, so a shop manager or owner can review them in a couple of minutes. If a recurring problem is caused by how the building is used, such as a mudroom without enough matting, we say so and suggest a practical fix.
