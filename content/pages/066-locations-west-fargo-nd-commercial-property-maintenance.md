## Two kinds of West Fargo property

West Fargo's growth, with population up 6.6% between the 2020 base and 2025 according to Census estimates, has produced two distinct property types that need different maintenance approaches.

### Newer commercial and multifamily buildings

Much of West Fargo's commercial and apartment stock was built recently. For these properties, maintenance is about protection:

- **Warranties.** Roofs, windows, equipment and finishes often carry warranties that depend on documented maintenance and timely reporting of defects.
- **Settling and early defects.** New buildings show early issues, such as sealant gaps, door adjustments, drainage surprises and cracking flatwork, in their first years.
- **Baseline.** A documented condition record from the start makes every future conversation, with contractors, insurers or buyers, easier.

### Working industrial and commercial properties

West Fargo's industrial areas hold warehouses, shops, contractor yards and dealerships where buildings take daily abuse: damaged wall panels, worn dock seals, failed exterior lights, cracked truck courts and doors that never quite close. For these properties, maintenance is about keeping operations running and stopping small damage from compounding.

## West Fargo's weather exposure

Open, wind-exposed sites on the city's edges see drifting snow, hard freezes and summer storms. The National Weather Service office in Grand Forks issues warnings for the area on all three. Seasonal walkthroughs focus on:

- **Late fall:** doors and weatherstripping, drains, exterior lighting, trip hazards before snow
- **Winter:** entry conditions, drifting against doors and vents, interior leaks from ice
- **Spring:** water around foundations, grading and downspouts, winter paving damage
- **Summer:** post-storm checks, sealants, exterior condition

## Tenant turnover in a growing market

West Fargo's commercial and multifamily properties turn over tenants as businesses grow and move. Each turnover is a chance to document condition, catch deferred issues and prepare the space for the next occupant. Our walkthroughs at move-out record what changed, our upkeep scope handles small items within our capabilities, and our reports give you a clear list for your trades. Owners who document turnovers consistently spend less time arguing about deposits and more time getting space back on the market.
