## Moorhead's commercial character

Moorhead had an estimated 46,533 residents in 2025, up 4.6% from its 2020 base, according to the U.S. Census Bureau. It is the county seat of Clay County, which had 1,306 employer establishments and about 16,800 employees in 2023. Moorhead's commercial base differs from Fargo's in a few useful ways:

- **A college town.** Minnesota State University Moorhead, Concordia College and M State's Moorhead campus shape the city's calendar, traffic and the businesses around them.
- **A traditional downtown and Center Avenue corridor**, with offices, financial institutions, government and service businesses.
- **Retail and service along the main highways**, including the US-10 and 8th Street corridors.
- **Healthcare and professional services** serving Clay County and the wider metro.
- **Strong community institutions:** churches, schools and civic buildings.

## What is different about serving Moorhead

### A different state

Moorhead is in Minnesota, so the rules that apply to our work there differ from North Dakota. Two examples that matter to buyers:

- **Employment rules.** Minnesota's Earned Sick and Safe Time law, in effect since January 1, 2024, requires paid sick and safe time for employees who work in the state. Vendors that ignore Minnesota employment rules can create disputes and turnover. We account for them.
- **Sales tax on services.** Minnesota taxes certain building cleaning and maintenance services. North Dakota generally does not tax services the same way. Moorhead invoices reflect whatever tax applies under Minnesota rules, so you are not surprised later. Check with your tax advisor about your own situation.

### Academic calendars

Businesses near MSUM and Concordia see sharp swings in traffic between semesters, summers and breaks. We adjust frequencies seasonally where that makes sense, rather than charging for the same service level year-round when the building is half empty.

### Older buildings

Downtown Moorhead and parts of the city's commercial core include older buildings with mixed flooring, masonry and aging fixtures. Assigned teams learn their quirks, and our maintenance service documents their condition. See [commercial property maintenance in Moorhead](/locations/moorhead-mn/commercial-property-maintenance).

### River and climate

Moorhead shares the Red River with Fargo. Spring thaw and flood seasons put lower levels and building envelopes at risk. Our guide to [Red River flood season property prep](/resources/red-river-flood-season-property-prep) applies on both sides of the river.
