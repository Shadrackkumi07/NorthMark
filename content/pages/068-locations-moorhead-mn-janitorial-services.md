## How Moorhead accounts differ

### Variable occupancy

Campus calendars, church schedules and civic meeting cycles mean many Moorhead buildings have uneven weekly and seasonal use. A church may need heavy cleaning after Sunday services and weekday events but little in between. A business near campus may be busy from September to May and quiet in summer. We build schedules around actual use rather than a flat nightly plan.

### Minnesota rules

Moorhead is in Minnesota, so different employment and tax rules apply than across the river:

- **Earned Sick and Safe Time:** Minnesota requires paid sick and safe time for employees who work in the state. Vendors who staff Minnesota sites without accounting for it create turnover and compliance risk.
- **Sales tax:** Minnesota taxes certain building cleaning and maintenance services. Moorhead invoices reflect whatever tax applies. Consult your tax advisor about your situation.

Our guide to [janitorial contract terms](/resources/janitorial-contract-terms) covers how tax and compliance should be handled in any cleaning agreement.

### Older buildings

Downtown Moorhead and established neighborhoods include older commercial and institutional buildings: masonry churches, mid-century schools and offices, older retail. Their finishes, layouts and fixtures need assigned teams who learn them.
