Northmark provides commercial property maintenance for building owners and property managers in Moorhead, Minnesota. We perform scheduled and seasonal condition walkthroughs, handle general upkeep tasks agreed in the scope, and deliver written reports with photos, while items that need a licensed trade go to your contractors with clear documentation. We pay particular attention to the issues that matter most on the Minnesota side of the river: older building envelopes, spring water and winter wear.

## Moorhead's commercial property mix

Moorhead's commercial properties cover a wide age range:

- **Downtown and Center Avenue buildings**, some decades old, with masonry walls, older roofs and windows, and mechanical systems that reward regular attention
- **Mid-century commercial and institutional buildings** in established neighborhoods
- **Newer retail and service buildings** along the US-10 and 8th Street corridors and on the city's growing south side
- **Small multi-tenant and mixed-use buildings** owned by local investors
- **Apartment buildings** serving students and young professionals

Many Moorhead owners are local individuals or small partnerships without dedicated maintenance staff. A scheduled program with clear reports gives them a professional view of their property without hiring.

## Moorhead-specific priorities

### The river

Moorhead and Fargo share the Red River of the North. Spring melt raises the river most years, and historic floods have made river season a permanent part of local property management. Lower levels, sump systems, grading, downspout discharge and foundation walls all deserve attention before and during spring. Our guide to [Red River flood season property prep](/resources/red-river-flood-season-property-prep) explains the checks we recommend.

### Older envelopes

Older Moorhead buildings often have aging sealants, masonry joints, flashing and windows that let water and cold in. Regular walkthroughs catch early signs: efflorescence, stains, drafts, ice formation at specific spots and interior water marks. For roofs and upper walls, drone imagery adds detail without ladders. See [drone roof inspection](/services/drone-roof-inspection).

### Winter wear

Long winters, ice melt and freeze-thaw cycles damage entries, sidewalks, curbs and paving. Doors and closers struggle in the cold. A late-fall walkthrough prepares the building and a spring walkthrough documents the damage.

## What we do not do

Northmark does not perform electrical, plumbing, HVAC, roofing, fire and life safety or structural work. We identify and document those needs for your licensed contractors. That keeps responsibilities clear and avoids hidden markups.

## Reports owners can actually use

A maintenance report is only useful if someone reads it. Ours are short and organized by priority: items needing urgent attention, items to schedule with a trade, items we handled within scope, and items to watch. Each finding includes a photo, a location and a plain description. For owners who live out of town, or who hold one or two Moorhead buildings as investments, that format provides a clear picture of the property without driving over for every visit. Over time the reports form a record that supports budgeting, refinancing and sale conversations.
